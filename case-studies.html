Case Studies

Expanding Community Health Access with Ulti-Care

Challenge: A regional health authority struggled with providing timely care to remote villages. Geographic barriers, manual data entry, and under-equipped Community Health Workers (CHWs) led to delayed diagnoses and poor patient outcomes.

Solution: HEATH deployed the Ulti-Care platform, equipping CHWs with offline-first mobile apps for patient registration, triage, and data collection. We integrated telehealth capabilities for remote consultations and established a central analytics dashboard for real-time monitoring.

Outcome: The initiative successfully bridged the last-mile gap. Patient travel time was reduced by 40%, administrative errors fell by 30%, and CHWs could manage 25% more cases effectively. The health authority gained unprecedented visibility into community health trends, enabling proactive interventions.

Key Metrics

  • 40% Reduction in Patient Travel
  • 25% Increase in Case Visits
  • 30% Fewer Admin Delays

National Health Supply Chain Optimization

Challenge: A national medical supplier faced chronic stockouts of essential medicines due to inaccurate demand forecasting and inefficient inventory management. This disrupted healthcare services and increased operational costs.

Solution: HEATH implemented a comprehensive supply chain solution featuring an intelligent rules engine for demand forecasting and automated inventory replenishment. We integrated real-time analytics to monitor stock levels, supplier performance, and delivery timelines.

Outcome: The new system cut stockouts by 35% within the first year and reduced carrying costs by 20%. Forecast accuracy improved dramatically, ensuring essential medicines were consistently available where needed most. The supplier transformed from a reactive to a proactive supply chain manager.

Key Metrics

  • 35% Reduction in Stockouts
  • 20% Lower Carrying Costs
  • 95% Forecast Accuracy

AI-Powered Analytics for a Financial Services Firm

Challenge: A private financial services firm needed to enhance its fraud detection capabilities and improve customer risk modeling without alienating legitimate clients. Their existing systems were slow and generated too many false positives.

Solution: HEATH developed and deployed a custom AI solution with a focus on ethical adoption. The system used machine learning to analyze transaction patterns in real-time, identifying suspicious activities with high accuracy. We also provided comprehensive training to their team on interpreting AI-driven insights and managing the system responsibly.

Outcome: The firm reduced fraudulent transactions by 50% while cutting false positives by over 60%. This not only saved money but also improved customer trust. The project demonstrated how AI can drive significant business value when implemented ethically and with a human-centric approach.

Key Metrics

  • 50% Reduction in Fraud
  • 60% Fewer False Positives
  • 100% Ethical AI Framework Adoption
